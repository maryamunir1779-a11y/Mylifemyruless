Why Aanchal

Aanchal started with a simple idea: a woman's wardrobe should not have to choose between tradition and comfort. We work with local weavers and small ateliers across Pakistan — gold zari necklines and hand-painted florals for the formal side, soft prints and easy shapes for the everyday one.

Every piece on this site is chosen and described by hand — no catalog filler, no stock photography. What you read here is what you'd be told standing in the shop.
