Our Story

Why Aanchal

Aanchal started with a simple idea: traditional Eastern wear doesn't have to be quiet. We work with local weavers and small ateliers across Pakistan — gold embroidery and rich colour for the formal side, playful prints for the everyday one.

Every piece on this site is chosen and described by hand — no catalog filler, no stock photography of the garments themselves. What you read here is what you'd be told standing in the shop.
